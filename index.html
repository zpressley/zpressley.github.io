About

I work in GTM and revenue operations — the layer between the go-to-market team and the systems they depend on.

Most of what I do starts as a question someone can't answer from a dashboard: why pipeline moved the way it did, where deals stall, which motion is actually working. Answering it usually means fixing the data model before the report, and the process before the data model.

I build outside of work too — currently a Postgres-backed fantasy baseball application wired to live MLB data, which is where most of my schema design and API-plumbing practice happens.
