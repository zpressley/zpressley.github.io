About

I work in GTM and revenue operations, the layer between the go-to-market team and the systems they depend on.

Most of what I do starts as a question someone can't answer from a dashboard. Why pipeline moved the way it did, where deals stall, which accounts belong to whom. Answering any of those usually means fixing the data model before the report, and the process before the data model. The last four years were Anaconda and TrustEngine: lead routing during a platform migration, territory design, deal desk, and a lot of CRM data that had to become trustworthy before anything built on it could be.

I build outside of work too. Pantheon Leagues is a fantasy baseball platform with live draft tooling and its own economy, and it's where most of my schema design and API-plumbing practice happens.
