Fantasy Baseball Platform

Three separate tools — a prospect database, a trade evaluator, and a draft app — rebuilt as one Postgres-backed application wired to live MLB data.

Role
Design & build
Timeline
2026 — ongoing
Stack
Postgres, Python, MLB data APIs
Status
In progress

The problem

The three tools each solved one piece of a fantasy season and none of them talked to each other. Prospect research lived in one place, trade math in another, and draft-day decisions in a third — so the same player existed three times, under three shapes, with no shared truth about who he was or how he was performing.

What I'm building

A single Postgres schema that treats players as the primary entity and everything else — prospect grades, trade valuations, draft board position, live season stats — as attributes hanging off it, synced against live MLB data.

  • One player identity, resolved across sources that don't agree on IDs or naming
  • A sync layer that keeps live stats current without re-ingesting the world every run
  • Trade and draft logic reading from the same tables, so valuations never drift apart
